The problem

Skill fades quietly

Payout math, game-protection awareness, and floor procedure all decay between refreshers. The decay is invisible right up until the moment it is expensive.
On the floor

Payout errors

Every mispaid hand is money leaving the property — and a pattern of them is a training gap that is not measured.

In procedure

Quiet drift

The distance between what the manual says and what the muscle memory does widens slowly, and never announces itself.

On paper

No evidence

When someone asks for proof that a team is trained, an attendance sheet is not a record of competence.

The gap

Most training answers “did they attend?” A readiness system has to answer “can they still do it — and how do you know?”

A.S.S.E.T. · Briefing for Pit Operations05
The scored drill

Read the felt. Do the math. Type the payout.

  • Payout drills — a live bet on the table layout. Type the dollar. This can be read as text or visual as seen in the example.
  • Multiple choice — rules, game protection, and procedure scenarios. Options are reshuffled every single time.
  • Difficulty is visible — the Easy / Medium / Hard tag reflects the level the engine has you at for that game.
  • Roulette and Craps offer a 2D / 3D table view, and Roulette scenarios are generated fresh each time — never numerically identical twice.
Why it cannot be memorised

Payout answers are computed from bet × ratio, not matched against stored text. The chip stack changes on every draw.

A.S.S.E.T. · Briefing for Pit Operations07
Adaptive difficulty & question selection

The drill adjusts to the person taking it

Difficulty moves on performance

  • Each person holds a level from 1 (Easy) to 3 (Hard), tracked independently per game.
  • Step up after three correct answers in a row at the current level.
  • Step down after two wrong in a row. Bounded — never below 1, never above 3.
  • Practice never moves it. Only scored drills change your level.
Difficulty does not change the points

A hard question is worth the same ten points as an easy one. Difficulty controls what you are asked, not what it is worth — so scores stay comparable across the team.

Every session is freshly built

  • Questions from your last three sessions are excluded, so the pool keeps turning over.
  • Maximum four of ten from any one game, and no more than two per procedure category — guaranteeing breadth.
  • No two payout questions with the same game and ratio, and no duplicate wording, in a single session.
  • Weaker games surface about 1.5× more often — a lean toward your gaps, not a fixation on them.
There is no “Quiz #1”

Sessions are assembled at the moment you press Start. There is no fixed set to pass around.

Practice & skill trainers

Unlimited practice: Extra practice never hurt anyone.

The Practice picker — any game, or a dedicated Skill Trainer in gold.
The Blackjack Strategy Trainer — call the play, learn the house rule.
  • Never affects score, cooldown, difficulty, certification, or the question statistics management reads.
  • Never blocked — practice stays open during the four-hour cooldown.
  • Dedicated trainers for Blackjack strategy and hand recognition in Caribbean Stud, Ultimate Hold'em, Let It Ride, and Three Card Poker.
  • Practice is logged — as evidence of effort, and it earns credit toward assigned remediation.
A.S.S.E.T. · Briefing for Pit Operations09
Instant feedback

The explanation is where the training happens

  • In practice, every answer is graded immediately — your answer against the correct one.
  • Each question carries a written explanation of the rule, not just the number.
  • In a scored drill the same review arrives at the end, on the results screen, for every question missed.
Why this matters for the floor

A wrong payout corrected in the moment, with the reasoning attached, is worth more than a classroom hour a quarter later.

Every rule, chart and walkthrough, in one place

Each game card opens its Rules, Videos, Calculator and — where available — Trainer tabs.
  • Rules and payout PDFs for every game, so the authoritative answer is one tap away on shift.

Team Dashboard

The command centre

  • The amber banner names everyone below the 20-session monthly target.
  • The purple banner names anyone in score decay — a drop of 15% or more comparing the last fourteen days against the fourteen before.
  • Department average, headcount, and how many are certified this month, at the top.
  • Every roster row opens that person's full detail page — the screen to have open during a coaching conversation.
Read the status column first

Deactivated accounts stay on the roster to preserve their history, and they still count toward “below target”.

Screens throughout use demonstration data — fictional names and seeded sessions. Layout, controls, and rules are live.

A.S.S.E.T. · Briefing for Pit Operations13
Weak Areas

Where the department actually struggles

  • Accuracy by game, the most-failed individual questions, and each person's weakest area.
  • Filterable by date range, person, and game — 30 days, 90 days, six months, or all time.
  • Bars are colour-coded: red below 60%, amber 60–75%, green above 75%.
  • Scored sessions only. Practice answers never enter these numbers.
Read the pair, not the number

A game at 55% over 400 answers is a training problem. The same 55% over nine answers is noise. Volume sits beside every rate for exactly this reason.

A.S.S.E.T. · Briefing for Pit Operations15
Remediation  ·  department heads only

From “they're weak at Craps” to a closed loop

Each row shows the drills + practice split against the target. The assign form pre-suggests the agent's weakest game from the last 90 days.
  • A head assigns a focus area with a note and a due date.
  • It appears on the agent's own dashboard with a button straight into focused practice on that game.
  • Progress counts qualifying drills plus practice credits — ten focused practice questions earn one credit.
The drill floor

An assignment can only auto-complete once at least one real scored drill has happened. Practice alone can never certify a remediation — assessment integrity is preserved by design.

Evidence & exports

“Prove this team was trained.”

Every management screen exports to Excel, honouring the filters on screen and the department you belong to.
The one that answers the question

Compliance Records export

Two sheets, taken from the Completion Tracker:
  • Summary — every person, sessions completed, required, status, and a plain Compliant yes/no column.
  • Session Detail — every completed session that month with date, time, score, and duration.
  • The recert exception notes travel with it, so an approved absence is explained inside the same file.
Taken monthly and filed consistently, it turns an audit request into a folder.

The department wall

Pit data stays inside Pit

Enforced in the database on every query — not hidden in the interface. It holds however a page is reached.
Shared across the wall
  • The question pool — a question written by a Surveillance supervisor is served to Pit Managers too and vice versa.
  • The games
  • The resources library — rules, videos, and calculators
Never shared
  • People, sessions, and answers
  • Audit entries and management notes
  • Leaderboards and team benchmarks
Benchmarks never mix

A Pit Manager is ranked only against Pit; a Surveillance agent only against Surveillance. Because the populations are different, a figure from a Surveillance report and a figure from a Pit report should never be set side by side and called a gap.

Security, privacy & data

A platform holding named performance data
has to be defensible

ControlHow it works
Row-level securityAccess rules live in the database. Role and department are re-derived on every query.
Audit logAppend-only. Drill-takers have no access at all. Never records passwords.
LockoutFive failed attempts in fifteen minutes. Every attempt recorded.
TimeoutAutomatic sign-out after thirty minutes idle.
Privileged actionsAccount creation, password resets and deletion run server-side with a key that never reaches the browser.
IP and device, and what it is for

Every session records an IP address and browser. Its purpose is integrity, not monitoring staff — it is how you confirm or clear a concern that one person drilled on another's account.

Retention is a decision management owns

There is no automatic purge. Sessions, answers, and audit entries accumulate indefinitely — which is what you want for training evidence, and what you must be deliberate about for privacy.

If the property's records policy requires a limit, that is a decision to take deliberately and document.
