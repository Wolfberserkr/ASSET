Welcome to A.S.S.E.T.

A.S.S.E.T. — Aruba Surveillance Skill Enhancement & Training — is the department’s web-based drill platform. It keeps your payout math, game-protection knowledge, and floor procedures sharp through short, scored gaming drills, and gives supervisors and directors a clear view of team readiness.

What you do here

  • Run drills — 10-question scored sessions drawn from seven casino games plus shared floor procedures.
  • Practice freely — unlimited, unscored practice with instant feedback, including dedicated skill trainers.
  • Track progress — scores, accuracy, weak areas, and your monthly certification status.

Who uses it

The platform serves two departments that never see each other’s data (the “department wall”): Surveillance and Pit. Everyone signs in the same way; what you see afterwards depends on your role.

RolePortalWhat they do
Agent (Surveillance)Drill portalRuns drills, practice, and trainers; tracks own progress.
Pit Manager (Pit)Drill portalSame drills as agents, labelled “Pit Operations”.
Supervisor / Shift ManagerManagement portalViews team performance and reports; can create & edit questions.
Director (Surveillance head)Management + AdminEverything a supervisor sees, plus Scorecard, Remediation, Audit Digest, and User Management.
Casino Manager (Pit head)Management + AdminSame head tools as the Director, scoped to Pit staff.
◆ The department wall
Surveillance staff and Pit staff never appear in each other’s dashboards, leaderboards, reports, or audit records. Questions, games, and resources are shared; people and their results are not.

Signing In

You log in with the Employee ID and password your administrator handed you — there is no public sign-up and no email required.

  1. Open assetdrills.com in any modern browser (works on desktop, tablet, and phone).
  2. Type your Employee ID exactly as issued — it is not case-sensitive.
  3. Enter your password and select Sign in. You land on your Dashboard automatically.
▲ Important — account lockout
After 5 failed attempts within 15 minutes the account is temporarily locked. Wait for the window to pass or ask your supervisor. Forgot your password? There is no self-service reset — “Forgot your password? Contact your Supervisor.” A head can set a new one for you in seconds.

Changing your own password

Once signed in, open Change Password from the sidebar (under Help). Enter a new password — the change is immediate and needs no approval.

▸ Automatic sign-out
For security the app signs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity. A department head can also force-log-out an active session; if that happens you’ll be returned to the login screen within about a minute.

The Drill Portal

This is what agents and pit managers use every shift. The left sidebar is your map: Dashboard, Drill, Practice, Resources, My History, plus How It Works and Change Password under Help.

4.1  The Dashboard

Your home screen summarises where you stand right now: sessions completed this month, your rolling average score, cooldown state, per-game accuracy, recertification progress, and how you rank against your team.

On the DashboardWhat it tells you
Sessions This MonthProgress toward the 20-session monthly requirement.
Avg Score · 14 daysYour recent form. Unlocks a trend line once you have a few sessions.
Days Left / CooldownDays remaining in the recert window and whether you can drill now.
Accuracy by GameRings showing how you’re doing per game — your weakest game is flagged.
Team LeaderboardYour rank within your department (Surveillance or Pit only).
◆ Assigned Practice
If a supervisor has given you a focus area, an Assigned Practice card appears at the top of the Dashboard with a Practice button (jumps straight to that game) and a Start Drill button. See §7 (Remediation).

4.2  Running a Drill

A drill is a 10-question, 10-minute scored session drawn from across all games and shared procedures. Faster, accurate sessions score higher (see §5).

Two question types

  • Payout drills — a bet is shown on the felt; you type the correct dollar payout. Validated to a 2-cent tolerance.
  • Multiple choice — rules, game-protection, and procedure scenarios; pick the correct option.

Good to know

  • Difficulty adapts to you (§5). The tag by the question shows Easy/Medium/Hard.
  • Roulette and Craps tables offer a 2D / 3D view toggle.
  • Leaving early marks the session abandoned — it scores 0 and doesn’t count.

4.3  Your Results

Finishing a session opens a full breakdown, so you learn from every miss.

4.4  Practice Mode

Practice is unlimited and completely separate from your record — no score, no cooldown, no effect on your stats or difficulty. Choose a single game, Procedures, or Mixed — All Games.

▸ Tip — practice with purpose
Use the accuracy rings on your Dashboard or the Weak Areas report to pick what to practise. Focused practice on an assigned game also earns credit toward any Remediation assignment (§7).

4.5  Skill Trainers

Beyond the games, dedicated trainers drill specific dealer skills — the Blackjack Strategy Trainer and Hand Recognition Trainers for Caribbean Stud, Ultimate Hold’em, Let It Ride, and Three Card Poker.

4.6  Resources

Everything you need for reference lives here: rules & payout PDFs, video walkthroughs, and a payout calculator, organised by game.

4.7  My History

A running log of your sessions with dates, status, score, accuracy, and time. Your monthly progress, best score, and average sit at the top.

4.8  Pit Operations

Pit managers use the exact same portal — same drills, scoring, cooldown, and certification — with the sidebar simply labelled Pit Operations and the team leaderboard scoped to Pit staff.

How Scoring & Certification Work

A few simple rules govern every scored drill. Knowing them helps you plan your shift and hit your monthly target.

Scoring

  • 10 points per correct answer (100 base for a perfect 10/10).
  • A speed multiplier is applied to the whole session at the end.
Finish timeMultiplier
≤ 5 minutes× 1.5
5 – 7½ minutes× 1.25
7½ – 10 minutes× 1.0

Maximum score per session: 150 (10 correct × 10 × 1.5).

Session structure

  • 10 questions, drawn across all games plus shared procedures.
  • 10-minute cap per session.
  • Weaker games surface a little more often, so drills target your gaps.
  • Recently-seen questions are skipped to keep sessions fresh.

Cooldown & recertification

RuleDetail
4-hour cooldownAfter each completed session you must wait 4 hours before the next scored drill. The Drill link in the sidebar shows a live countdown. Practice has no cooldown.
20 sessions / monthEach drill-taker must complete 20 sessions in a calendar month to stay certified. Only completed sessions count.
Missed targetFalling short flags the account and notifies the department heads in-app.

Adaptive difficulty

Each game tracks your level (1 Easy → 3 Hard) independently:

  • 3 correct in a row at your current level → step up.
  • 2 wrong in a row → step down.
  • Bounded between Easy (1) and Hard (3). Practice never changes it.
▲ Important — abandoned sessions
Leaving a drill before you finish records it as abandoned: it scores 0 and does not count toward your 20. Finish the session — even a low score beats an abandon.

The Management Portal

Supervisors, shift managers, and department heads sign in the same way but land in the management portal. All views are scoped to your department and every list can be filtered and exported to Excel.

6.1  Team Dashboard

The command centre: team averages, who is behind on sessions, score-decay alerts, and a searchable roster with per-agent detail.

6.2  Agent Detail

Drill into one person: score trend, per-game averages, and full session history including IP/device for each sitting.

6.3  Completion Tracker

Certification at a glance: who has hit 20 sessions this month and who hasn’t, with automated flagging. You can record exceptions (e.g., approved leave).

6.4  Weak Areas

Where the team struggles — accuracy by game and the most-failed questions, filterable by date range, agent, and game.

6.5  Question Stats & 6.6 Question Editor

Question Stats shows how each question is performing (times shown, accuracy) for your department. The Question Editor lets supervisors and heads add or edit questions in the shared pool.

◆ Question pool health
A game needs at least 30 active questions to be considered ready; the portal warns when a game drops below that line.

6.7  Audit Log

An append-only record of significant actions — logins, completed sessions, question edits, account changes — for the department. Agents have no access.

6.8  Notifications

The sidebar bell collects the alerts that need attention — agents who missed last month’s target and agents whose scores are decaying.

Department Heads — Extra Tools

The Director (Surveillance) and Casino Manager (Pit) see four additional pages under Reports and Admin. Supervisors and shift managers do not.

7.1  Scorecard

Month-over-month department KPIs — average score, sessions, active drill-takers, recert-compliance rate, and accuracy — with trend arrows, sparklines, and a per-game accuracy matrix.

7.2  Audit Digest

A period rollup (24h / 7d / 30d / 90d) of department activity: totals by action, most-active users, notable/security events, and a failed-login summary — with exact counts that the 500-row Audit Log can’t always show.

7.3  Remediation

Assign a drill-taker a focus — a specific game or Procedures — with a note and a due date. The assignment appears on that person’s Dashboard, and progress is tracked automatically.

◆ How an assignment completes
Progress counts qualifying drills plus practice credits (every 10 focused-practice questions = 1 credit). It auto-completes when the target is reached and at least one real drill has happened — practice alone can never certify completion. A head can also mark it complete or cancel it by hand.

7.4  User Management

Heads manage their own department’s accounts end-to-end — no IT ticket required.

ActionWhat it does
Create userAdds an account in your department with the role you choose.
Reset passwordSets a new password directly — the model here is “hand credentials over in person”.
Force logoutEnds the user’s active session within about a minute (temporary).
Deactivate / ReactivateThe reversible block that preserves all history. A deactivated user cannot log in.
Delete permanentlyOnly offered for accounts that have never run a session; otherwise the app deactivates instead.
▲ Important
Every account action is written to the Audit Log, and heads can only act on their own department — never on themselves or the other head. Passwords are never recorded in the log.

Tips, Shortcuts & Excel Exports

Navigation

  • The left sidebar is always your map; the highlighted item is where you are. On a phone, tap the ☰ menu icon.
  • Your Employee ID and Sign out live at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • In the drill portal the Drill link is greyed out during cooldown and shows the remaining time.

Keyboard shortcuts (Skill Trainers)

Blackjack Strategy: H Hit · S Stand · D Double · P Split. Hand trainers use the on-screen option keys shown under each hand.

Excel exports (management)

  • The Export Excel button appears on the Team Dashboard, Agent Detail, Completion, Weak Areas, Question Stats, Audit Log, Scorecard, Audit Digest, and Remediation.
  • Filter first — by date range, agent, and game where offered — then export; the file reflects your filters.
▸ Tips worth keeping
  • Beat the clock, but don’t rush. The ×1.5 multiplier needs a sub-5-minute finish — valuable only if you stay accurate.
  • Warm up in Practice. It never touches your score, cooldown, or difficulty — the perfect place to shake off rust before a scored drill.
  • Finish every drill. Abandoned sessions score 0 and don’t count toward your 20.
  • Read the misses. Every wrong answer comes with the correct answer and an explanation — the fastest way to improve.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

QuestionAnswer
I forgot my password.There’s no self-service reset. Ask your supervisor or department head — they can set a new one for you immediately.
My account is locked.Five failed logins in 15 minutes triggers a temporary lock. Wait for the window to clear, or ask a head.
The Drill button is greyed out.You’re in the 4-hour cooldown after a completed session. The countdown is on the button. Practice is always available.
I was signed out unexpectedly.Either 30 minutes of inactivity, or a head force-logged the session. Just sign back in.
Does Practice affect my score or ranking?No. Practice writes nothing to your record — no score, cooldown, or difficulty change.
My session says “abandoned”.It was left before finishing. It scores 0 and doesn’t count. Always complete the 10 questions.
Why don’t I see the other department?By design. Surveillance and Pit are walled off from each other’s people and results.
Can I use my phone?Yes — the app is fully responsive. On payout drills the keyboard opens only when you tap the amount field, so the table stays visible.
